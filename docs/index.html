National Parcel Coverage · July 2026

Where US land records are free to collect

Every county keeps a public record of who owns each piece of land. Most publish it online, each in its own format. This map shows, county by county, whether that record could be collected from a free public source and put into one national store.

What this page shows. County totals only: how many parcel records are in the store, where they came from, and how often each field is filled. It does not show any parcel, owner name, or address.

What "filled" means. The share of a county's records where the field has a value. It does not measure whether the value is correct.

Limits. Counts are from the July 2026 build. A source date is recorded for about a third of the counties in the store. Puerto Rico's 78 municipios are recorded but are outside the store.
