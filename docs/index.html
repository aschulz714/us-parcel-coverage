Where US land records are free to collect

Every county keeps a public record of who owns each piece of land. Most publish it online, each in its own format. This map shows, county by county, whether that record could be collected from a free public source and put into one national store. Zoom in on a green county to see its parcel outlines, drawn live from the county's own server where that server still answers.

What this page shows. County totals from the store: how many parcel records it holds, where they came from, and how often each field is filled. Zoomed in close, it also draws parcel outlines and parcel numbers straight from the county's own public server. Those do not come from the store, and this page never asks for an owner name or an address.

What "filled" means. The share of a county's records where the field has a value. It does not measure whether the value is correct.

Limits. Counts are from the July 2026 build. A source date is recorded for about a third of the counties in the store. Puerto Rico's 78 municipios are recorded but are outside the store.
